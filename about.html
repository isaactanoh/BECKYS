Our Story

Becks Cleaning Services was founded in 2018 with a simple mission: to provide exceptional cleaning services that make a real difference in people's lives.

What started as a one woman operation has grown into a trusted team of cleaning professionals serving homes and businesses throughout the Greater Accra Region.

Miss Rebecca, our founder, began her cleaning career while working part-time to support her education. She quickly realized her passion for transforming spaces and creating clean, healthy environments for families and businesses.

Today, we're proud to have expanded our team while maintaining the personal touch and high standards that set us apart from the beginning.
